    How to print & bind
    1. Print the PDF double-sided (flip on the short edge).
    2. Keep the sheets in order. Every 4 sheets = one pliego.
    3. Fold each pliego in half.
    4. Stack the pliegos in order and bind (staple, sew, or glue the spine).
